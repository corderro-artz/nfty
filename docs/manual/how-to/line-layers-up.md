# Line one layer up with another

Eyes have to land on a face. A hat has to sit on a head. **Reference layers** show you the rest of
the character while you draw one piece of it.

## Turn one on

In the Ingredient Editor, scroll the right-hand panel to **IN THIS RECIPE** and tick any layer:

![The reference layer list, with layers marked UNDER and OVER](../images/reference-layers-light.png#only-light)
![The reference layer list, with layers marked UNDER and OVER](../images/reference-layers-dark.png#only-dark)

Each one composites at its real depth:

- **UNDER** — sits below the layer you are editing, drawn normally
- **OVER** — sits above it, drawn dimmed so it cannot hide your work

The counter at the top right (`4 / 6 on`) tells you how many are showing.

## See the real thing

**Ghost above** dims the layers above you. **True color** shows the honest composite instead — what
the asset will actually look like. Switch to it to check a fit, switch back to keep drawing.

## Borrow from the Kitchen

Below the recipe's own layers, **FROM THE KITCHEN** lists loose `.igt` files in your
[Kitchen folder](kitchen.md). Use it to line art up against a layer that is not in this Recipe —
a shared body you are keeping outside the book, say.
